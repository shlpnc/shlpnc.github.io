    Schedule performance index (SPI)

    Tasks completed ÷ tasks planned to finish, at each month end. 1.00 means on plan, below 1.00 means behind.

    Overdue backlog trend

    Tasks past their baseline finish and not yet completed, at each month end. Falling is good.

    Project health scorecard

    Rated by SPI as of the data date: on track 0.95 or more, at risk 0.85 to 0.95, behind below 0.85. A project needs 5 tasks due before it is rated. Select a row to filter.
